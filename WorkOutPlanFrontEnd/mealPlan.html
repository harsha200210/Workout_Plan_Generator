🔥 Your Meal Plan

🔥 Calorie Goal: 2,800 - 3,200 kcal

💪 Protein Goal: 100-120g per day (1.8 - 2.2g/kg)

🥑 Macronutrient Split:

  • 40% Carbs: Rice, Oats, Bread, Potatoes, Fruits
  • 30% Protein: Chicken, Eggs, Fish, Beef, Dairy
  • 30% Fats: Nuts, Avocado, Olive Oil, Peanut Butter

🥗 6 Meals a Day Plan

🕗 Meal 1 – Breakfast (700 kcal)
  • 4 whole eggs + 2 egg whites (30g protein)
  • 2 slices whole wheat bread
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 banana
  • 1 glass full-fat milk
🕙 Meal 2 – Morning Snack (450 kcal)
  • 50g oats with 1 tbsp honey
  • 10 almonds + 10 cashews
  • 1 scoop whey protein (if available)
🕛 Meal 3 – Lunch (800 kcal)
  • 1.5 cups cooked rice
  • 150g chicken breast or fish
  • 1 cup vegetables
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
🕒 Meal 4 – Pre-Workout (400 kcal)
  • 1 boiled sweet potato or 1 apple
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
🏋️‍♂️ Post-Workout Meal (450 kcal)
  • 1 scoop whey protein (if available)
  • 250ml full-fat milk
  • 1 tbsp honey
🕡 Meal 5 – Dinner (700 kcal)
  • 1.5 cups cooked rice
  • 150g beef or chicken
  • 1 cup vegetables
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
🕙 Meal 6 – Before Bed (400 kcal)
  • 1 glass warm milk
  • 10 almonds
  • 2 slices whole wheat bread with peanut butter

💡 Additional Tips

  • ✅ Drink at least 3-4L of water per day
  • ✅ Eat every 2-3 hours
  • ✅ Train heavy (progressive overload)
  • ✅ Get at least 7-8 hours of sleep
  • ✅ Use whey protein if needed to meet protein goals
